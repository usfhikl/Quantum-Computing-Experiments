# Quantum Ice Cream Experiment

## Idea
Using a quantum circuit (Hadamard gate) to simulate randomness between two outcomes:
- Vanilla (|0⟩)
- Chocolate (|1⟩)

## Concept
A qubit is placed in superposition using a Hadamard gate, then measured multiple times to observe probabilistic outcomes.

## Goal
To visualize quantum randomness using a simple real-life analogy (ice cream flavors).

## Tools
- Qiskit
- Python
- Matplotlib
